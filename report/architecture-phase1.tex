\section{Network topology}
Two physical Macs share one Wi-Fi LAN. Ubuntu backends are \textbf{UTM guests inside Your Mac}, not extra Wi-Fi hosts. Manohar reaches them only as \texttt{10.7.12.104:3001} and \texttt{:3002}. Guests (\texttt{10.0.2.x}) are not pingable from the LAN. Clients never type a backend address. HTTPS always goes \textbf{client $\to$ Manohar :8443 first}. The dashed arrow is a DNS A-record only.

\section{Why this satisfies the brief}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.15em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Four \emph{roles} exist. Hardware may be combined; the backends remain two processes on two ports.
  \item Emulated VLAN is hypervisor hosting, not a NAT protocol we implemented.
  \item Ports 8080/8443 are permitted. The zone is \texttt{.test}, never \texttt{.local}.
  \item Evidence: \texttt{dig} to 10.7.2.38 via 10.7.12.104\#53; TLSv1.3; issuer \texttt{localmesh Local Root CA}; \texttt{X-Backend} A/B.
\end{itemize}
